## Full description

Every body is different. Kenko is a health companion that sizes your goals to *you* — your
body, your fitness level and what you want to work on — and keeps all of your data private on
your phone.

**MOVE**
• Start a live workout for 59 activities — running, walking, cycling, gym and weight
  machines, football, basketball, tennis, swimming, yoga, martial arts and many more
• A big timer, a live notification with your time and calories, and a Finish button when
  you're done — or log a session afterwards
• Activity rings for Move, Exercise and Mindful minutes
• Daily cardio load: see if you're training below, at or above your usual
• Step count and daily activity

**EAT WELL**
• Log meals by scanning a barcode or searching a food database, with portion scaling
• Calories, protein, carbs and fat against goals sized to your body
• One-tap water tracking

**FEEL BETTER**
• Private check-ins: mood, stress, energy, sleep, emotions, journal and gratitude
• Guided breathing and meditation with spoken guidance
• Gentle support resources when you're having a hard day

**HEART & HEARING**
• Measure your pulse with a 30-second guide, and log resting heart rate and blood pressure
• Safe-listening guide: how long different sounds are safe each day

**UNDERSTAND YOURSELF**
• Weekly, monthly and yearly trends with insights that link your habits to your mood
• Encouragement built on your own progress — never made-up statistics about other people
• Badges, points and levels that reward effort

**YOURS, AND PRIVATE**
• No account, no ads, no tracking — your data stays on your phone
• Move your history between iPhone and Android, and import from Apple Health, Samsung Health
  and Google Fit
• Works with your phone's Google or iCloud backup

Light and dark mode. Food search uses the free Open Food Facts database.

Kenko is a wellness app, not a medical device, and doesn't give medical advice.
