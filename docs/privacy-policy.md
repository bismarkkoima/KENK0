# Kenko Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 21 October 2026_

Kenko is a health and wellbeing companion app. This policy explains what information Kenko
handles and how. In short: **your health data stays on your phone.**

## Information Kenko stores

Kenko stores the information you enter or allow it to read, **only on your device**, in a
private database that other apps cannot access:

- **Profile:** name (optional), birth year, sex (optional), height, weight, fitness level and
  goals.
- **Activity:** workouts (type, time, duration, distance, effort, estimated calories), steps
  read from your phone's motion sensor, and daily activity totals.
- **Food & water:** meals and their nutrition values, and water intake.
- **Heart health:** resting heart rate and blood pressure readings you enter or import.
- **Mind:** mood check-ins, stress, energy and sleep ratings, emotions, journal entries and
  gratitude notes, and meditation sessions.
- **Settings:** reminders, badges and preferences.

Kenko does **not** have its own servers or accounts. We (the developer) never receive, see,
sell or share your health data.

## Information that leaves your phone

- **Food search and barcode scanning:** your search text or the scanned barcode number is sent
  to [Open Food Facts](https://world.openfoodfacts.org), a free public food database, to look
  up nutrition values. No other information is sent with it. Camera images are processed on
  the phone to read the barcode and are never saved or uploaded.
- **Phone backups:** if you have turned on Google backup (Android) or iCloud Backup (iPhone),
  your phone's operating system may include Kenko's data in that backup, under your Google or
  Apple account and their privacy policies. You can turn this off in your phone's settings.
- **Files you export:** when you export a backup or transfer file, you choose where it goes
  (for example Google Drive or email). That file contains your health data, so keep it
  private.

## Permissions

All are optional, and Kenko works without them:

- **Motion & fitness / Physical activity:** to count your steps.
- **Notifications:** for reminders and the live workout notification.
- **Camera:** only to scan food barcodes.

## Imports

When you import data from Apple Health, Samsung Health or Google Fit export files, the file
is read on your phone only and the data is added to Kenko's on-device database.

## Your choices and deleting data

You can edit or delete individual entries in the app. Uninstalling Kenko deletes all of its
data from your phone (copies in your own phone backups or exported files are managed by you).

## Children

Kenko is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from
them.

## Medical disclaimer

Kenko is a wellness tool, not a medical device, and does not provide medical advice,
diagnosis or treatment. Calorie, energy and cardio-load figures are estimates. If you are in
crisis or have a medical emergency, contact your local emergency number.
